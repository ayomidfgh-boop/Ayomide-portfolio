About

Technology should move a business forward.

I am an AI engineer and full-stack developer based in Lagos. My practice sits at the intersection of three things: AI agents, business automation, and dependable web systems — designed around a clear business problem, not technology for its own sake.

I build research agents that turn a company website into a structured brief, automation workflows that replace repetitive manual steps, and full-stack applications that connect a responsive interface to real server-side logic, validation, and deployment.

The problems I solve are practical: slow lead research, scattered business information, manual data processing, and workflows that depend on memory and one person. Every build stays tied to a measurable operating outcome, and AI output is always separated from verified facts and kept under human review.

Process

A clear path from problem to system.

Projects stay grounded in the workflow they are meant to improve.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the business problem, users, constraints, and desired next action.

  2. 02

    Research

    Gather the inputs, data, and external signals the system will work with.

  3. 03

    Design

    Structure the workflow, data, interface, and human decision points.

  4. 04

    Build

    Develop the frontend, backend, and automation around real inputs and outputs.

  5. 05

    Integrate

    Connect forms, APIs, databases, notifications, and external tools.

  6. 06

    Test

    Validate inputs, outputs, routes, and failure cases with automated tests.

  7. 07

    Deploy

    Ship a production-ready system with a clear deployment path.

  8. 08

    Improve

    Review what works, document limitations, and improve from evidence.
