01 About

A focused practice

Technology should move a business forward.

I am an AI engineer and full-stack developer based in Lagos. My practice sits at the intersection of three things: AI agents, business automation, and dependable web systems. I design systems around a clear business problem, not around technology for its own sake.

I build AI-assisted workflows that reduce repetitive manual work, research agents that turn a company website into a structured brief, and full-stack applications that connect a responsive interface to real server-side logic.

What I specialize in — agentic AI and AI agents, AI automation, full-stack web development, business automation, API and backend development, and intelligent business systems. The problems I solve are practical: slow lead research, scattered business information, manual data processing, and inefficient workflows.

Why businesses use these systems — every build is tied to a measurable operating outcome, each claim is backed by working, verifiable code, and AI output is always separated from verified facts and kept under human review.
